The room is dark. The movie is the light.

A blue-violet auditorium black, one marquee-gold action colour, and a single bold move: the colours of the moods you pick spill into the page as a soft "screen glow". Everything else stays quiet so posters can be the colour.

Mood first, genre secondMood is the primary interaction on every screen; genre is a filter, never the headline.
Poster-first, chrome-lightCards have no boxes. Metadata sits under the art, never on it.
Honest by designNo invented ratings or availability. Empty and error states say what to do next.
World cinema by defaultDiversity is built into ranking; languages show their native names.
